About ZungiDill

ZungiDill is a phone app made by Numerita LLC, a small software company in Sheridan, Wyoming. It helps you check your own credit report and send your own dispute letters, without paying anyone every month.

Why it exists

Credit repair companies charge every month for letters you have the right to send yourself, and most people never see what was sent. ZungiDill puts that work in your hands. You read every letter, and you decide what goes out.

How the app stays honest

  • Your report stays on your phone. We never see it.
  • There is no account, and the app never asks for your Social Security number.
  • It only works with your own credit report.
  • It will not tell you to call something wrong when it is right.

How we write our guides

Every page in Learn is checked against federal law and official pages from the Federal Trade Commission and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau. Each one lists its sources and the date it was last checked, and we update them when the rules change. They are general information, not legal advice.
