What ZungiDill is

  • Self-help software for reviewing your own credit reports, deciding what you believe is inaccurate, and drafting letters you send yourself.
  • Not a credit repair organization, credit counseling service or law firm. It does not give legal, financial or credit advice.
  • It does not contact credit bureaus, creditors, debt collectors or government agencies for you, and it never sends anything on your behalf.
  • The free review points out items worth checking against federal reporting rules. You decide what is wrong.
  • Letters are built from set wording plus your answers. Read every letter and change anything that is not true for you.

No guarantee of results

Credit bureaus and the companies that report to them decide every dispute. No outcome, removal or score change is promised. Accurate, current information cannot lawfully be removed. Removing inaccurate or outdated negative items may help a score, but no change can be guaranteed.

Your responsibilities

  • You must be at least 18 and live in the United States.
  • Use ZungiDill only for your own credit file. A report in another person's name is not accepted.
  • Dispute only items you believe in good faith are wrong, incomplete, outdated or not yours. Disputing accurate items can get a dispute rejected as frivolous.
  • Use identity theft letters only if you are a real victim. A false identity theft report is a crime.
  • Keep copies of everything you send, and send copies of documents, never originals.

Your free rights

You can dispute directly with the credit bureaus and the companies that report to them for free, without ZungiDill. Free credit reports are at AnnualCreditReport.com. Credit freezes are free under federal law.
