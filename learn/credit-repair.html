Credit repair companies: what they can and cannot do

Last updated October 22, 2026 · Checked against official sources

The quick answer

A credit repair company can only do what you can do yourself for free: dispute mistakes on your credit report. By law it cannot charge you before the work is done, it has to give you a written contract, and you can cancel within three business days (Credit Repair Organizations Act, 15 U.S.C. 1679b, 1679d and 1679e). Nobody can remove true information from your report.

What the law says

  • They cannot charge you before they finish the work they promised.
  • They have to give you a written contract that spells out the work, the cost and how long it takes.
  • You can cancel within three business days of signing, for free.
  • They cannot tell you to lie on a credit application or to the credit bureaus.

Red flags

  • They want money up front.
  • They promise to remove everything, even items that are true.
  • They tell you not to contact the credit bureaus yourself.
  • They offer a new credit identity or a CPN. That is a scam, and using one can be a crime.
  • They dispute everything on your report, true or not.

Common questions

Is credit repair worth it?

For most people, no. Everything they can legally do, you can do yourself for free in about the same time.

What is a CPN?

A credit privacy number, sold as a way to get a fresh credit file. It is a scam, and using one to apply for credit can be a federal crime.
