Goodwill letter for a late payment: a free sample

Last updated October 22, 2026 · Checked against official sources

The quick answer

A goodwill letter asks a lender to remove a late payment that really happened, as a favor. They do not have to say yes. It works best when the late payment was a one time slip, your account is current now and the rest of your history is good. It costs nothing to ask.

When it makes sense

  • One or two late payments on an account that is otherwise on time
  • The account is paid up now
  • There was a real reason, like a job loss, a hospital stay, a move or an autopay change

It is less likely to work for an account that is still behind, a long run of missed payments, or a debt that was sold to a collector.

Goodwill letter or dispute?

A dispute says the item is wrong. A goodwill letter says the item is right and asks for a favor. If the late payment did not really happen, send a dispute letter instead. Never claim something is wrong when it is not.

Sample goodwill letter

[Your full name]
[Street address]
[City, State ZIP]

[Today's date]

[Lender name]
[Customer service or credit reporting address]

Re: Goodwill request for account ending in [last four digits]

I have been a customer since [year]. In [month and year] I missed a payment because [one honest sentence, for example: I lost my job for two months]. I caught up as soon as I could, and my account has been paid on time since then.

That one late payment is now holding me back as I try to [buy a car, rent an apartment, get a better rate]. I am asking you, as a goodwill gesture, to remove the late payment from my credit reports.

Thank you for considering this.

Sincerely,
[Your signature]
[Your printed name]

Who to send it to

Send it to the lender, not the credit bureaus. Look for a credit reporting or customer service address on your statement or the lender's website. You can also call and ask for a goodwill adjustment. If the answer is no, you can try again later, after more months of on time payments.

Common questions

Does a goodwill letter hurt my credit?

No. Asking does not change your report. The worst answer is no.

Can I send a goodwill letter to a collection agency?

You can, but with collections a pay for delete offer usually gets further. See how to dispute a collection on your credit report.
