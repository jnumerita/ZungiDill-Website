How it works

You do each step yourself, on your phone or computer. Your letters go to the credit bureaus: Equifax, Experian and TransUnion, the companies that keep your report.

  1. Add your credit report

    Get your free report and open it in ZungiDill. A photo of a paper copy works too.

  2. Check each item

    Go through your report one item at a time and mark anything that isn't right.

  3. Read and mail your letter

    ZungiDill writes the letter from what you marked. You read it, then print and mail it.

  4. Track the 30 days

    Enter the date it was delivered. We'll remind you when the bureau's 30 days are up.

How long does it take? Bureaus usually have 30 days to answer. If you're planning to apply for something, start now.
