When ZungiDill helps

You were told no, or offered a high rate

Lenders decide from your credit report. ZungiDill shows you what they see, so you can check it for mistakes.

You paid a credit repair company and nothing changed

You never saw what they sent. Here you read every letter before it goes out, and you mail it yourself.

You pay cash for most things

That works until you need a lease or a loan. Your report follows you either way, so make sure it's right.

You don't know where to start

Start with your free credit report. ZungiDill reads it with you and shows you what to look at first.
